1. Revenue Growth and Peak Performance
   
 -2022 was the strongest year, with fluctuative transaction performance cause by a high demand in April, July, and October.
 -Q2 2022 saw the highest revenue ($1.29B in May 2022), making it the best-performing period.
 -December 2021 - March 2022 ($420M Profit) also maintained strong sales, after peak sales in November 2021($420M) Profit.
2. Fluctuative Trend in 2022

-A sales anomaly and significant fluctuation occurred in 2022, particularly in Q2-Q4, with April ($216M), July ($202M), and September ($54M) marking the highest profit months.
-The Q3 and Q4 revenue decline suggests a major downturn, likely caused by external market conditions, reduced consumer demand, or internal operational shifts.
-The Q4 profit uprising suggest a major brand of new product being released, based on the amount of Mobile & Tablets (1024 unit) being ordered.
-In April there’s a big gap between profit and revenue, likely caused by Woman fashion with 172 out of 526 total unit being sold.
-While most products almost equally contributed in July with Soghat, Mobile & Tablets, and Superstar as the highest unit being sold.
3. Key Takeaways & Recommendations

-Investigate the causes of the 2022 fluctuation (e.g., market changes, new brand product, internal factors).
-Leverage high-performing periods (e.g., Q4 of both years) to refine marketing and sales strategies.
-Reassess business strategy for 2023, focusing on pricing, promotions, discount and customer engagement to regain momentum.
